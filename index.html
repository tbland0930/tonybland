About Me

I obsess over the details others overlook.

I'm a hands-on Product UX Design Leader from sunny Jacksonville, Florida with over 15 years of experience leading cross-functional teams through the entire design process — from ideation to execution.

I love cars, fashion, art, music, food, and of course good design. I'm inspired by many people — from the amazing Italian automotive designer Walter de Silva to the legendary Apple product designer Jony Ive. I find beauty in almost everything, but I'm also a problem solver, so usability pioneers such as Dr. Jakob Nielsen equally inspire and influence the work I do.

I thrive in environments where I can advocate for modern practices and foster a culture of design excellence. I consider the nuances of design to be crucial when creating truly intuitive products.
